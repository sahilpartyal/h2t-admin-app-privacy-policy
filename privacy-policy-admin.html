01 Introduction

H2T Admin ("we", "our", or "us") is the back-office application for Here2There, used by ground transport and chauffeur operators to manage bookings, dispatch drivers, set pricing and issue invoices. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information on iOS and Android.

H2T Admin is a staff application, not a passenger app. You need an account issued by your company administrator to sign in — accounts cannot be created inside the app. Because the app exists to run a business, it necessarily handles real personal data: yours as a staff user, and that of your company's customers, drivers and suppliers.

By using H2T Admin, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you disagree with any part of it, please discontinue use of the application.

02 Information We Collect & How We Use It

Account & Sign-in

Your credentials are sent to the Here2There server to verify your identity. On success:

  • An authentication token is returned
  • It is stored in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore
  • It authorises your later requests
  • It is deleted when you sign out

Business & Operational Data

Records you create are stored on the Here2There server:

  • Customers — names, phones, emails, addresses
  • Drivers, suppliers and vehicles
  • Bookings, quotes and booking messages
  • Pricing, promo codes, invoices, payments
  • Incident notes

Used only to provide the service to your company — never for advertising or profiling.

Contacts (Optional)

So you can add a customer without retyping their details, the app can read your device address book.

  • Entirely optional — you may decline
  • Read only at the moment you import one
  • Never uploaded, indexed or synced in bulk
  • Revocable anytime in device Settings

Photos You Attach

Where the app lets you attach an image, it opens your operating system's own photo picker.

  • Only the image you pick is read
  • It is compressed and attached to that record
  • The app has no standing access to your photo library

Push Notifications

Where enabled for your deployment, your operating system issues an anonymous device token so operational alerts can reach you.

The token does not identify you personally and cannot be used to read anything on your device.

Technical Data

As with any networked app, requests to our server include standard technical details:

  • IP address
  • App version
  • Device platform

These may appear in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

03 Data We Don't Collect

H2T Admin contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no behavioural tracking of any kind. Specifically:

  • No device location tracking. The app displays maps but does not request location permission and does not track your phone's position. Addresses come from what you type or select — never from your GPS.
  • No card or bank details. The app records that a payment happened and for how much. We never receive, process or store card numbers, CVVs or bank credentials.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID) and no ad networks.
  • No microphone access, no photo library scanning, and no reading of your other apps' data.
  • No selling of data to anyone, under any circumstances.
04 Who Controls the Data

This distinction matters, because most of the personal data in the app is not your own:

  • Your employer — the transport operator whose account you sign in with — is the data controller for the customer, driver and supplier records in the system. They decide what is collected, why, and how long it is kept.
  • Here2There provides the software and hosting, acting as a data processor on your employer's instructions.

If you are a passenger, driver or customer of a transport company and want to know what they hold about you — or want it corrected or erased — please contact that company directly. They control your data, and we can only act on their instruction.

06 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • Within your company — records you create are visible to other authorised staff on your company's account. That is the purpose of a shared dispatch system.
  • With the service providers in Section 05, strictly to make the app function.
  • Where the law requires it — a valid legal request or court order, or to protect against fraud, abuse or a threat to someone's safety.
  • On a change of ownership — if the business is transferred, data may transfer with it, subject to this policy.
07 Data Retention & Deletion

Operational records are retained while your company's account is active, and afterwards for as long as your company's retention rules and its legal or tax obligations require. Invoices and payment records in particular usually must be kept for several years by law.

Signing out — deletes the stored authentication token from your device
Uninstalling the app — removes all app data from the device
Server-side deletion — contact your company administrator, or write to us using the details in Section 12

Note that signing out or uninstalling does not delete records from the server — those belong to your company, not to your handset.

08 Security
  • Your authentication token is held in the platform's hardware-backed secure storage — the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore — not in plain application storage.
  • All traffic to the Here2There server is sent over HTTPS.
  • Access requires an account issued by your company; there is no public sign-up.
  • Access to operational records is scoped to your company's account.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your account by using a strong password, not sharing your login, and keeping your device locked and up to date.

09 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your personal data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to how it is used, or receive it in a portable form.

If you are a staff user, contact us or your company administrator. If you are a customer or driver of a transport company using H2T Admin, contact that company — as explained in Section 04, they control your data and we act on their instructions. We respond to verified requests within the period required by applicable law.

10 Children's Privacy

H2T Admin is a professional business tool intended for adult staff of transport operators. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child's data has reached us, contact us and we will remove it.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page. For significant changes, we may notify you via an in-app notice or through your company administrator. Continued use of H2T Admin after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
